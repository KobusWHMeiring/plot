Why I do this

Systems that stay out of the way.

So much of the world's important work happens quietly — growing food, restoring rivers, tending gardens. The people doing it are already busy. They rarely have time to set up the planning, the record-keeping, and the numbers that would make the work easier and the impact visible.

I've been on both sides. I've managed a farm and gardened in my community; I've also led products in the finance sector and built software. That combination — knowing the work first-hand, and knowing how to build for it — is what I bring.

The Work

Three systems, in real use.

01

Harvester

Urban garden & landscaping operations

A landscaping team's day lived on paper job cards — lost, wet, or stuck in the van. Good work went unbilled and clients were left in the dark. I built a platform that turns a photo of the form into a clean, verified record.

  • document_scanner AI handwriting extraction
  • mark_email_read Magic-Link client portal
  • analytics Margin & job costing
  • map Mapping & scheduling

Django · Python · Celery + Redis · Gemini Vision · Leaflet

02

River

Field-operations system for river rehabilitation

A field team planned river rehabilitation on paper, with no clear view of who did what, where, or what it all added up to. I built a platform that helps them plan the work, log the day, and see the impact they're making.

  • map GIS mapping with lifecycle stages
  • calendar_today Weekly / monthly / daily planners
  • edit_note Smart visit logging & photos
  • analytics Impact dashboards & Excel export

Django · Python · PostgreSQL · Leaflet · vanilla JS

03

Farm

Farm management for a regenerative farm

A regenerative farm's knowledge lived on paper lists, WhatsApp chats, and in people's memories. I built a calm, local-first system that keeps track of the work, the money, and the land — offline, on the farm's own hardware.

  • account_balance Double-entry finance & reconciliation
  • agriculture Cattle grazing & paddock rest
  • flight Drone mapping
  • view_kanban Team todo, routines & offline logging
