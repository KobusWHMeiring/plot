The Philosophy

Run your field operation like a tech company.

In fintech, a lost decimal point is a crisis. On the land, a lost job card is "business as usual." I disagree.

I bring systems thinking to the field — find the bottleneck, automate the grunt work, and surface the numbers usually reserved for companies with a whole operations team.

The Work

Three production systems

01

Harvester

Urban garden & landscaping operations

A landscaping team's day lived on paper job cards — lost, wet, or stuck in the van — so billables leaked and clients were left in the dark. I built a platform that turns a photo of the form into verified, billable, client-ready data.

  • document_scanner AI handwriting extraction
  • mark_email_read Magic-Link client portal
  • analytics Margin & job costing
  • map Mapping & scheduling

Django · Python · Celery + Redis · Gemini Vision · Leaflet

02

River

Field-operations system for river rehabilitation

A field team planned river rehabilitation on paper, with no single view of who did what, where, or the cumulative impact. I built a Django platform that turns the whole operation into a planned, tracked, measurable workflow.

  • map GIS mapping with lifecycle stages
  • calendar_today Weekly / monthly / daily planners
  • edit_note Smart visit logging & photos
  • analytics Impact dashboards & Excel export

Django · Python · PostgreSQL · Leaflet · vanilla JS

03

Farm

Farm management for a regenerative farm

A regenerative farm's operational knowledge lived on paper lists, WhatsApp chats, and in people's memories. I built a local-first platform that tracks the work, the money, and the land — offline and on the farm's own hardware.

  • account_balance Double-entry finance & reconciliation
  • agriculture Cattle grazing & paddock rest
  • flight Drone mapping
  • view_kanban Team todo, routines & offline logging
