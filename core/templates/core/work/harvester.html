Case Study

Harvester

Software for high-end landscape maintenance.

A landscaping team's day lived on paper job cards — lost, wet, or stuck in the van. Good work went unbilled, and clients never quite knew what had been done. I built a platform that turns a photo of the paper form into a clean, verified record — so the work is billed and the client is kept in the loop.

Django · Python · Celery + Redis · Gemini Vision · Leaflet · PostgreSQL.

The problem

Field teams are brilliant at horticulture — but the paperwork that follows them isn't where their time is best spent. Job cards get lost, wet, or sit in the van for days. Extra bags of compost and unexpected plantings quietly fall off the invoice. And clients genuinely want to know what happened in their garden, but the message gets lost in the noise.

The solution

Keep the paper — let the software do the typing. Gardeners keep using the forms they already know, snap one photo, and the AI reads the handwriting and turns it into a clean record. No new app to learn, and a reliable, billable record at the other end.

What it does

document_scanner AI handwriting engine

Vision AI reads handwritten job cards — even the messy scrawl — and extracts inputs, plantings, and tasks into a structured, human-in-the-loop verification queue.

mark_email_read "Magic Link" client portal

A secure, time-bound link emailed after every visit. Clients see the summary, rate the visit, and request wishlist plants — no login required.

analytics Operational intelligence

Automatic nursery shopping lists aggregate every client request, and margin analysis shows exactly what inputs are used per site versus what's billed.

calendar_today Scheduling & calendar sync

A 14-day rolling sync with Google Calendar maps visits to team leaders and generates operational prep sheets for each crew.

map Spatial intelligence

Interactive Leaflet mapping of client locations, density clusters, and manual pin-drops for plots where geocoders fail.

timeline Crop lifecycle tracking

Biological data (days-to-maturity, harvest windows) projects the "active garden" state, with a 12-month Gantt view of growth and harvest.

In the field

How it's built

  • Service-layer pattern — complex logic lives in services.py; views stay thin.
  • Performance first — select_related/prefetch_related throughout to avoid N+1 queries.
  • Async by design — external calls (Gemini extraction, email) run in Celery tasks, never inside a web request.
  • Schema-first — data model migrations are backwards-compatible; safe-enrichment migration patterns.
  • Testing — integration tests cover the "golden flow" (upload → extract → verify → wishlist → shopping list).

Outcomes

  • In daily use by a commercial garden-maintenance operation.
  • Turned Monday-morning handwriting deciphering into a quick, verified review.
  • Closed the gap: extra inputs and unplanned plantings now reach the invoice.
