Case Study

Farm

Farm management for a regenerative farm.

A regenerative farm's operational knowledge lived on paper lists, WhatsApp chats, and in people's memories — it didn't scale, couldn't calculate ecological or financial flows, and held institutional memory hostage. I built a local-first platform that logs the work, reconciles the money, and maps the land — on the farm's own hardware, offline where the signal dies.

Django 5.1 · Python · PostgreSQL · HTMX · Leaflet · Huey · Gemini OCR.

The problem

Farm knowledge was scattered across paper, chats, and memory. It couldn't answer the questions that matter — did we make money, did we build soil, did we empower the community — and it lived in whoever happened to write the note.

The solution

A "digital nervous system" for the farm: one place to log work, track animals and assets, reconcile money, and preserve knowledge. It tracks three forms of capital — financial, ecological, and social — rather than optimising for yield alone, and runs as an appliance on farm hardware rather than a SaaS dependency.

What it does

account_balance Finance & reconciliation

A unified double-entry ledger with a ~40-account chart of accounts, bank-statement reconciliation with a learning suggestion engine, invoicing, a cashbook, and a finance dashboard that shows which farm enterprises are healthy.

agriculture Cattle movement & grazing

Rotational grazing tracked as moves between paddocks, with per-paddock rest periods (grazing / resting / ready) rendered as coloured map borders and the herd's pin auto-relocated.

flight Drone mapping

Georeferenced drone imagery processed client-side in the browser, rendered as map overlays, and auto-linked to grazing moves — tying aerial imagery to management decisions.

view_kanban Team todo, planner & routines

A Kanban board with prioritisation scoring, a week-ahead planner, and recurring "heartbeat" routines in active and passive modes — so daily friction stays low.

map Map & spatial model

A unified GeoJSON model for every mappable asset — points, fences, paddock polygons — with water infrastructure (tanks, pumps, outlets) drawn directly on the map.

wifi_off Offline rapid logger

A mobile PWA using IndexedDB, camera, and audio recording that survives Wi-Fi dead zones and syncs later — so field capture never blocks on signal.

In the field

How it's built

  • Service-layer architecture — complex logic lives in ~60 service modules, not models or views; cross-service imports are lazy to avoid cycles.
  • Performance discipline — "prefetch or perish": subquery annotations and query budgets tuned for a Raspberry Pi footprint.
  • Testing at scale — ~456 Python tests, 8 Playwright end-to-end specs, and 48 UAT checklists.
  • Architecture guard — a custom static-analysis linter holds the codebase at 0 errors.
  • Local-first & white-label — an "appliance", not a SaaS dependency: it runs on farm hardware and is built so any regenerative farm can adopt the same software.

Outcomes

  • Live and in daily use on a working farm.
  • A production snapshot shows 56 active entities and a ~40-account chart of accounts.
  • 81 open tasks, with 16 completed in the prior week (17.5 hours logged) — real daily adoption by the team.
  • Runs offline in field dead zones and on farm hardware.

Stack

Django 5.1 · Python · PostgreSQL · HTMX · Leaflet · Huey · Gemini OCR — running on farm hardware.
