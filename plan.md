3. The Solution (The 3 Pillars)
Pillar A: The AI Handwriting Engine
"Keep the Paper. Automate the Data."
Your gardeners don't need to learn an app. They continue using the paper forms they know. They snap one photo, and our Vision AI reads the handwriting (even the messy scrawl) and digitizes it instantly.
Benefit: Zero training time for field staff.
Pillar B: The "Magic Link" Client Portal
"A 'Waitrose' Experience for your Clients."
Stop sending text messages. After every visit, your client receives a branded, professional email with a secure link.
Visit Summary: Exactly what was done.
Feedback Loop: Clients rate the visit (1-5 stars) so you can catch issues early.
Wishlist: Clients can request plants and "upsells" directly from their phone.
Pillar C: Operational Intelligence
"Know your Numbers."
Automatic Shopping Lists: We aggregate every client request into a precise nursery shopping list for the week.
Profit Tracking: See exactly how many inputs (fertilizer, compost) are being used per site vs. what you are billing.
